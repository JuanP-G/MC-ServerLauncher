FEATURES

Everything your server needs, without opening a terminal

What are normally config files, commands and loose folders are buttons here.

Create servers in 2 minutes

Pick a version, accept the EULA and you're done: it downloads the official JAR and generates the world for you. Vanilla, Paper, Purpur, Forge, NeoForge and Fabric.

Mods and plugins from Modrinth

Search and install mods without leaving the app, filtered by your version and server type. Every download is verified.

Automatic backups

Saves your world before every start, on stop and every hour while people are playing, without interrupting the game. Keeping as many copies as you want, with one-click restore.

Play with friends over the Internet

Built-in Playit.gg tunnel: share an address and they join from anywhere. No router port forwarding.

Console, players and stats

The memory the server really holds and its live TPS, a console search, and each player’s profile: when they joined, what they said and what they mined. OPs, bans and whitelist with buttons instead of commands.

It recovers from crashes on its own

If the server dies it restarts by itself and tells you why, reading the crash report. With desktop notifications.

It uses only the memory it needs

The app decides how much RAM each server may use and Java gives back what it does not need: an empty server goes from 3.5-4.7 GB to about 1 GB. It learns from how you use it and, if the PC runs short of memory, tightens sooner.

It warns you when it is at its limit

A card shows the server’s TPS and a band appears only when something is wrong for a while. “See solutions” offers one-click fixes: lower the distances, more memory, or mods that speed it up.

It stops by itself when empty

It stops the server when nobody is left and starts it when a friend tries to join, without wasting RAM or CPU.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions

How do I make a Minecraft server on my PC?

Install MC-ServerLauncher, click “+ New”, choose “Create a new one”, then the version and type (Vanilla, Paper, Purpur, Forge, NeoForge or Fabric), accept the Minecraft EULA, and the app downloads the official files and generates the world for you.

How do I host a Minecraft server for free?

By hosting it yourself on your own computer. MC-ServerLauncher downloads the official server, configures it and keeps it running, and the built-in tunnel gives it a public address so your friends can join without a monthly fee or router port forwarding.

Is a free server on my PC better than a paid one?

On your PC you pay nothing, there are no player or mod limits imposed by a plan, and the world is yours: the files are on your disk. In exchange, it is only available while your computer is on. A paid one is always online, but it costs a fee and usually caps RAM and mods.

Is it free and safe?

Yes. It is free and open source: you can review the code on GitHub. Every download (Minecraft, Java, mods and the app's own updates) is checked against its official checksum before running. The app's own updates are also signed with a key that isn't on GitHub.

Do I need to forward router ports to play with friends?

No. The app includes a Playit.gg tunnel: it gives you a public address you can share, without touching your router settings.

Does it work on Linux and macOS?

Yes. There is an AppImage for Linux and a DMG for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), plus the Windows installer.

Can I install mods and plugins?

Yes, from the built-in Modrinth browser, which automatically filters by your Minecraft version and your server type.

What happens if the server goes down?

It restarts by itself (up to three attempts) and shows you why, reading Minecraft's crash report, and notifies you as well.
